How we work

A process built for partnerships.

Fixed-price projects or monthly retainers · pricing on request

  1. 01

    Discover

    A short call to understand your client, constraints and what "done" looks like. We map scope, risks and the shortest path to shipped.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    You get a written plan — milestones, stack decisions, a comms cadence and a fixed price or retainer shape that fits the engagement.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos from a working build. Your team reviews in staging while we keep scope visible and surface risks early.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We ship, monitor and hand over: docs, repo access and a walkthrough session. Your team owns it — or we keep it on retainer.
